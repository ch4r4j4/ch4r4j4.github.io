1. Scope

Cuaderno Docente is a tool that helps teachers organize institutions, courses, students, schedules, attendance, grades, activities, conduct records, observations, and class diaries. This policy describes the features found in the reviewed version of the app. It must be updated if the app, its providers, or its practices change.

2. Information stored

Teachers may enter institution data (name and, optionally, address, phone number, academic year, and color), course data (name, schedule, classroom, and evaluation settings), student data (first and last names and optional student code), enrollments, and groups. The app can also store attendance and dates, grades, activities, academic periods, weighting settings, lesson plans and planned tasks, conduct records with severity and optional comments, free-form student observations, and class diary notes.

This information may identify students and may relate to minors. Cuaderno Docente is intended for teachers, not for students as app users. Teachers should enter only information they are authorized to manage and avoid adding sensitive information that is not necessary for their work.

3. Google account

If a teacher signs in with or links Google, the app receives a Firebase identifier, name, email address, and profile photo if available. These are used to identify the account, link local data, and enable synchronization. The app also allows users to continue as a guest; in that case, academic data remains on the device and account synchronization does not start.

4. Storage and synchronization

Academic data is first stored in a local SQLite database on the device. If Google is linked, academic records are also synchronized with Cloud Firestore in a space associated with that account. The project's Firestore rules limit read and write access to the authenticated user whose identifier matches that space. Synchronization allows records to be recovered on other devices when signing in with the same account.

Google/Firebase infrastructure may process technical information needed to provide authentication, storage, and synchronization. The responsible party should review the terms and settings of these services before publication.

5. Advertising

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), with a banner and rewarded ads associated with report generation. The reviewed development configuration has test ad units enabled. The advertising SDK may process device information, advertising identifiers, and ad interactions depending on its configuration and Google's policies. Before launch, the responsible party should confirm the UMP consent form, applicable consent requirements, and Google Play Data safety disclosures for the production configuration.

6. Exported files

Teachers can generate PDF reports, Excel reports, and a JSON backup file. The app prepares the file locally and opens the system's sharing or saving options. If the teacher selects another app or service, that third party receives the file under its own practices. Teachers decide with whom to share reports and backups.

7. Providers and recipients

  • Google/Firebase Authentication, for Google sign-in.
  • Google Cloud Firestore, to synchronize data when an account is linked.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), to load ads when the feature is active.
  • Apps or services selected by the teacher in the system menu when exporting or sharing files.

No feature that publishes academic data to other teachers or users was found.

8. Retention and deletion

In guest mode, data remains in the app's storage on the device until the user deletes it using the device's system tools, uninstalls the app, or restores a backup that replaces the data. When synchronization is enabled, records also remain in Firestore.

The reviewed code does not currently implement a way to request or perform deletion of a Google account and all of its synchronized records. Therefore, an effective deletion timeframe cannot yet be confirmed. Do not publish this policy until this process is implemented and tested both in the app and through an external web resource.

9. Security

The app separates local data by user and uses authentication for synchronization. Firestore rules restrict access to each authenticated user's space. Teachers should protect access to their device and Google account.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features or providers change. For privacy questions or requests, contact charajatp@gmail.com.

The final version must identify the app's responsible party, state an effective date, and match the actual practices and Google Play disclosures.
