Thank you for using Himnario Adventista Reforma. This application has been developed by Mantequilla Studio.

1. Information We Collect

This application does not require registration or user accounts. However, we automatically collect certain technical data for advertising and analytics purposes:

  • Device IDs (Google Advertising ID)
  • Crash logs
  • App performance diagnostics

This data does not include personally identifiable information such as name, email address, or phone number.

2. How We Use the Data

The collected data is used for:

  • Advertising: Displaying relevant ads through Google AdMob
  • Analytics: Improving app performance and stability
  • Diagnostics: Identifying and fixing errors

3. Sharing Data with Third Parties

The collected data is shared with:

  • Google AdMob (for advertising)
  • Google Play Services (for analytics and diagnostics)

For more information on how Google uses this data:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services

4. Advertising

This application displays ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on user activity.

Users can manage their ad preferences at:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Reset the Advertising ID from: Android Settings → Google → Ads

5. Data Encryption

All data is transmitted securely through encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Data Deletion

This application does not provide a direct way for users to request data deletion, because:

  • We do not store personally identifiable data
  • Advertising IDs can be reset by the user from Android settings
  • Google AdMob automatically deletes advertising data after 90 days of inactivity
  • Analytics data is automatically deleted according to Google's retention policies

7. Children's Privacy

This application is designed for use by people of all ages, including children.

COPPA Compliance: This application does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

8. Permissions

The application may request:

No other sensitive permissions are requested.
